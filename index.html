— I
About the Movement

A Crisis of
Foundation

There is a crisis happening quietly among men today. Not a crisis of ambition — it is a crisis of foundation.

Men are building without knowing who they are, deciding without emotional grounding, and leading without the tools to sustain what they carry. A MADE MAN exists to change that. And to ensure the next generation of men never has to start from scratch.

Through our annual conference, youth engagement initiatives, mentorship programmes and community outreach, we create spaces where men can be honest, boys can be shaped, and young people can discover who they are before the world tells them who to be.

Because a man who is whole on the inside builds better on the outside — and a nation is only as strong as the men, and the boys becoming men, who lead it.

The Convener

Temitope
Ajijola

Convener, A MADE MAN  ·  Founder, TAELF

A dynamic leader with over 17 years of experience across aviation, technology and business — a man who has consistently chosen the harder, more purposeful path.

Beyond his professional world, Temitope is deeply invested in the development of men and the shaping of the next generation of leaders. His commitment to men's health — physical, emotional and spiritual — is not a talking point. It is a conviction lived out in every room he walks into.

As Convener, he does not simply organise events. He builds rooms where men can be honest, grow intentionally and leave equipped to lead.

"A truly made man is not a finished product. He is a man committed to the process."

Every year, A MADE MAN Conference creates a room unlike any other — where men show up, sit down, and go deep. No performance required. No pretending allowed.

HE-MOTION is a conversation about the emotional lives of men. The feelings we carry, the weight we don't talk about, and how we can lead with the fullness of who we are — not just the parts that look strong.
